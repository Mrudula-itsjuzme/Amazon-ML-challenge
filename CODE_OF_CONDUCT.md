# Code of Conduct

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully and professionally.

We welcome technical disagreement, constructive criticism, and good-faith questions. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and sharing others' private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation to protect contributors and users.

To report conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through a contact channel they explicitly publish on their GitHub profile. Avoid public reports containing sensitive personal information.
